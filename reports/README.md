## 🎯 Key Findings Summary

### Critical Insights:
1. **Policy-Driven Growth (2016-2021):** 34pp increase through strategic interventions
2. **Platform Competition:** Telebirr dominance (70%) vs emerging M-Pesa competition
3. **Gender Gap Persistence:** 15pp gap remains despite progress
4. **Infrastructure Correlation:** Strong link (r=0.85) between 4G and inclusion
5. **Growth Slowdown:** Critical deceleration from 15%+ to <2% annual growth

### Business Impact:
- **Success:** Ethiopia achieved remarkable inclusion growth through coordinated policy
- **Challenge:** 2021-2024 slowdown threatens 2027 targets (70% inclusion)
- **Opportunity:** Strategic interventions can restart momentum
- **Urgency:** Next 18 months decisive for trajectory correction
