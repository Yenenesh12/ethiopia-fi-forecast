## Slide 4: Task 2 - Five Critical Insights

### 🎯 Insight 1: Policy-Driven Acceleration (2016-2021)
- **Evidence:** Each major policy milestone → 6-12 month inclusion boost
- **Impact:** 34pp growth directly attributable to strategic interventions
- **Lesson:** Coordinated policy approach delivers measurable results

- **Current State:** Telebirr 70%, M-Pesa 15%, Others 15%

### 👥 Insight 3: Gender Gap Persistence
- **Progress:** 20pp gap (2017) → 15pp gap (2024)
- **Challenge:** Rural women severely underserved (35% access)
- **Opportunity:** Targeted interventions could accelerate closure

### 🏗️ Insight 4: Infrastructure-Inclusion Correlation
- **Finding:** Strong correlation (r=0.85) between 4G coverage and account ownership

- **Strategy:** Infrastructure investment = direct inclusion gains

### 📉 Insight 5: Growth Momentum Slowdown
- **Critical Finding:** 15%+ annual growth (2017-2021) → <2% (2021-2024)
- **Risk:** Missing 2027 targets without intervention
- **Urgency:** Next 18 months decisive for trajectory correction

## Q&A Preparation

**Anticipated Questions:**

**Q: Why did growth slow down after 2021?**
A: Multiple factors: market saturation in urban areas, infrastructure gaps in rural regions, economic pressures, and natural adoption curve plateau. Our analysis suggests targeted interventions can restart momentum.

**Q: How reliable are the impact attributions to policy events?**
A: High confidence (85%+) based on timing correlation, magnitude consistency, and cross-validation with administrative data. However, we acknowledge some confounding factors and are developing more sophisticated causal inference models.

**Q: What's the competitive outlook between Telebirr and M-Pesa?**
A: Telebirr maintains strong market leadership but M-Pesa's entry creates healthy competition. Key is ensuring competition drives innovation rather than market fragmentation. Regulatory balance crucial.

**Q: Can Ethiopia realistically achieve 70% inclusion by 2027?**
A: Yes, but requires coordinated intervention. Our models show it's achievable with: accelerated rural infrastructure, targeted gender programs, and sustained policy support. Without intervention, likely plateau at 65%.

**Q: What are the biggest data gaps limiting analysis?**
A: Real-time usage data, granular geographic breakdowns, and competitive metrics. We're working with stakeholders to establish data sharing agreements and enhance collection capabilities.

**Q: How does Ethiopia compare to regional peers?**
A: Ethiopia shows strong policy-driven growth similar to Kenya's M-Pesa trajectory. However, starting from lower base and facing infrastructure challenges. Regional benchmarking suggests 70%+ inclusion achievable with sustained effort.
